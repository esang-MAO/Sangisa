Sangisa

Take a song apart. Make it anew.

Drop a kit here

A kit is the kit.json + slices/ folder the sangisa CLI writes, or the zip the Build a kit GitHub Action produces. Everything stays in your browser.

  1. Try the demo. A kit built by the real pipeline from a generated 16-bar song (no copyrighted audio).
  2. Build your own. In the repo, open Actions → Build a kit, choose Run workflow, and paste a direct link to a song you have the rights to. It takes about 15 minutes on GitHub's CPUs.
  3. Review it here. Download the sangisa-kit artifact from the finished run and drop the zip onto this page.

Tap a pad to play it. Keys: Z X C V bottom row … 1 2 3 4 top row. Loops keep playing until you tap them again.

Every candidate in the song

Solid marks are on pads, faint ones are backups. Click one to hear it, then Put on pad to swap it onto the selected pad.

Releasing music with uncleared samples can require permission from the rights holders. Only build kits from audio you own or have permission to sample.
